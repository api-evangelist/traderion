# Traderion

> Traderion combines technology and gamification to create digital learning platforms for banks and financial institutions. Its products are a gamified sales and trading simulator (equities, FX, bonds, and commodities with AI-powered feedback), a MiFID II regulatory-compliance training platform built as a virtual simulated bank, and a risk-profiling platform for detecting risky behavioral patterns within organizations.

Traderion is a Seedcamp portfolio company. It does not currently publish a public
